# Originals Interiors

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent international homeowners, property developers, architects, and hospitality operators commissioning high-touch residential, commercial, and branded spaces in Marbella and across Europe, the Middle East, North Africa, and the United States. They arrive with strong expectations but limited time. They need a studio that can interpret their taste, coordinate every discipline, and deliver a finished space without friction.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Originals Interiors as an experienced, international, turnkey interior design studio. It must let the work establish desire, make the full-service offer immediately understandable, and turn confidence into qualified project enquiries. Success means visitors can recognize the studio's point of view, explore complete project stories, understand the breadth of delivery, and start a conversation without navigating a dense catalogue.

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, assured, tactile. The voice is warm and direct, with the composure of an established studio and the human ease of a team that manages complicated projects every day. The emotional outcome is trust first, aspiration second, and calm throughout.

## Anti-references

- Generic beige luxury websites that remove all personality in the name of restraint.
- Theatrical black-and-gold styling, glossy gradients, glass panels, and faux exclusivity.
- Dense WordPress portfolio grids, miniature thumbnails, and navigation that exposes every page at once.
- Fashion-editorial imitation built from italic serif headlines, tiny monospace labels, and rule-heavy magazine layouts.
- Motion used as spectacle, bounce, or delay instead of helping visitors notice the work.

## Design Principles

1. **The work leads.** Use Originals Interiors photography as the primary proof of taste, range, and quality.
2. **Space signals confidence.** Give each image and idea room to register; reduce navigational and decorative noise.
3. **Warmth without cliché.** Express Marbella through light, material, planting, and generous pacing, never through coastal motifs.
4. **Turnkey clarity.** Make the path from concept to installation easy to understand while preserving a bespoke tone.
5. **Motion reveals craft.** Animation should uncover composition, material, and sequence without competing with photography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visual distinction takes priority over formal certification. The implementation still preserves semantic structure, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image alternatives, sufficient text contrast, responsive type, and a reduced-motion fallback so the experience remains usable without diluting the art direction.
